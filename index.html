About Web3 Hub

Founded in 2021 in Kas, on Turkey's Mediterranean coast. What started as local meetups grew into Web3 META Hub, connecting builders from Riyadh to Dubai, Istanbul to the Far East. Today, our hub bridges investors, developers, and innovators across the Middle East, Turkey, and Africa -- building the future of decentralized communities.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Web3 Hub?

Web3 Hub is an innovation and collaboration hub based in Kas, Antalya, Turkey. We bring together blockchain developers, entrepreneurs, investors, and enthusiasts to build the decentralized future through education, events, and community.

Where is Web3 Hub located?

We are located in Kas, Antalya, on Turkey's stunning Mediterranean coast. Kas is a historic town known for its Lycian heritage, crystal-clear waters, and growing international community.

How can I join?

Reach out to us via Telegram or email. We welcome builders, creators, and anyone passionate about Web3 and decentralized technology. Join our Telegram group to stay updated on events and opportunities.

Do you host events?

Yes. We organize hackathons, conferences, speaker panels, workshops, and community meetups throughout the year. Our events attract participants from around the world.

What is Web3 META Hub?

Web3 META Hub is the expanded network covering the Middle East, Turkey, and Africa. It represents our vision of connecting Web3 communities across these regions through a shared hub in Kas.
