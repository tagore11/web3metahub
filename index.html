About Web3 Hub

Founded in 2021 in the beautiful coastal town of Kas, Antalya, Web3 Hub started as a grassroots community for blockchain enthusiasts on Turkey's Mediterranean coast. What began as local meetups quickly grew into Web3 META Hub -- a global network spanning the Middle East, Turkey, and Africa.

Today, our Web3 Hub connects investors, developers, and innovators from Riyadh to Dubai, from Istanbul to the Far East. We are building the future of decentralized communities, right here in Kas, Turkey.
