Our Story

About Web3 META Hub

Founded in 2021 in Kaş, on Turkey's Mediterranean coast. What started as local meetups grew into Web3 META Hub, connecting builders from Riyadh to Dubai, Istanbul to the Far East. Today, our hub bridges investors, developers, and innovators across the Middle East, Turkey, and Africa -- building the future of decentralized communities.

Our Vision

From a Kaş Co-working Space to a Global Web3 Network

Web3 META Hub was established as an innovation and collaboration hub for Web3 technologies and projects in the Middle East, Turkey and Africa (META) regions. We first introduced the META concept at Alibaba Cloud Summit 2022.

Starting from Web3 Hub Kaş in 2021, we grew into a network that connects investors and developers from Riyadh, United Arab Emirates, and Far East ecosystems. In 2023, we partnered with ETH Riyadh, Dubai Gitex, Moscow Web3 communities, and organized ETHDevCon Istanbul at Bahçeşehir University with our Chinese DAO partners.

Our mission is simple: provide the infrastructure, community, and partnerships that Web3 builders need to ship. No noise. Just build.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Web3 META Hub?

Web3 META Hub is an innovation and collaboration hub for Web3 technologies covering the Middle East, Turkey, and Africa (META) regions. Based in Kaş, Antalya, we bring together blockchain developers, entrepreneurs, investors, and enthusiasts through residencies, hackathons, and community events.

Where is Web3 META Hub located?

Our physical hub, Web3 Hub Kaş, is located in Kaş, Antalya, on Turkey's stunning Mediterranean coast. Kaş is a historic town known for its Lycian heritage, crystal-clear waters, and growing international builder community.

How can I join?

Reach out to us via Telegram or email. We welcome builders, creators, and anyone passionate about Web3 and decentralized technology. Join our Telegram group to stay updated on events and opportunities.

Do you host events?

Yes. We organize hackathons, conferences, speaker panels, workshops, and community meetups throughout the year. Our events attract participants from around the world, with highlights including the Preda Hackathon and ETHDevCon Istanbul.

What does META stand for?

META stands for Middle East, Turkey, and Africa. It represents our vision of connecting Web3 communities across these regions through our hub in Kaş and our network of partners from Riyadh to Dubai, Istanbul to the Far East.
